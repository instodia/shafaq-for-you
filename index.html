DigiShakti Aadhaar e-KYC & Scheme Guidelines 📘

Everything you need to know about completing Aadhaar authentication, resolving data mismatches, and receiving your tablet/smartphone.

1

College Data Upload

Students do not need to manually register. Your college or university nodal officer uploads your enrollment data to the DigiShakti portal directly.

2

MeriPehchaan (e-Pramaan)

If your status shows Pending, log in via MeriPehchaan with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number to complete instant OTP identity authentication.

3

Device Distribution

Once verified and department locked, eligible final-year and technical students are scheduled for device distribution by district administration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What should I do if I get "Invalid Enrollment Number"?

Contact your college administrative office or DigiShakti nodal officer to confirm the exact enrollment format uploaded to the UP government portal.

Q: My name in Aadhaar is different from college registration.

If there is a name/DOB mismatch, update your Aadhaar card at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra and notify your college coordinator to resubmit verification.

Q: Is there any fee for registration or device?

No. The Swami Vivekanand Yuva Sashaktikaran Yojana tablet/smartphone is completely free of cost provided by the UP Government.
